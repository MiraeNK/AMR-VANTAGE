Use case: logo-brand.
Design one new standalone corporate industrial robotics emblem for FMR VANTAGE, an AGV/AMR fleet coordination software. Only the EMBLEM, absolutely no text or lettering. This is a replacement for a rejected forked-route icon that looked odd. New direction: a compact, authoritative, memorable symbol built from two interlocking precision-engineered geometric modules, an ordered coordinated fleet rather than a pictorial road intersection. The silhouette should be almost square with restrained 45-degree chamfers. Use two complementary angular ribbon forms wrapping a central square negative space, with clean deliberate separation between the modules, a feeling of a stylized interlocking F/M monogram, and subtle forward motion. Strong timeless industrial corporate identity, sophisticated like established automation instrumentation brands, completely original. Not just a V. No Y junction, no pitchfork, no branching terminals, no robot face, no gear, no circuit, no arrows, no tiny decorative parts, no border container.
One solid vivid industrial blue #075BCC only. Flat true solid fills, no gradient, no highlights, no shadows, no texture, no 3D or isometric perspective. The icon must be simple enough to redraw exactly with fewer than 30 vector path points and remain legible at 24 pixels. Transparent background. Present one balanced emblem with ample margin, no sheet of alternatives, no labels.
